About Solapur Weaves

Solapur Weaves are renowned handwoven textiles from Solapur, Maharashtra. Famous for their durability, vibrant colors, and comfort, they reflect India’s deep-rooted weaving tradition passed down through generations.

History & Heritage

The Solapur weaving industry dates back over 150 years and is globally known for Jacquard weaving. Solapur Weaves hold a Geographical Indication (GI) tag, ensuring authenticity and preserving India’s cultural heritage.
